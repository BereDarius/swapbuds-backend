## 5. Data Retention

### Account Data:

- **Active accounts:** Retained while active + 30 days after deletion
- **Deleted accounts:** Deleted immediately (unrecoverable after 30 days)
- **Backups:** May exist for 90 days for disaster recovery

### Messages & Chats:

- **Trade messages:** Deleted 1 year after trade completion
- **Support tickets:** Deleted 1 year after resolution
- **Dispute data:** Retained 2 years (legal requirement)

### ID Verification Documents:

- **APPROVED verifications:** Document deleted immediately after verification
- **REJECTED verifications:** Document retained 30 days (for user resubmission)
- **PENDING verifications:** Deleted if user deletes account
- **Legal holds:** Retained if required by law

### Analytics Data:

- **Individual-level data:** Deleted after 6 months
- **Aggregated data:** Retained indefinitely
- **Logs:** Deleted after 90 days
- **Error tracking:** Deleted after 1 year

### Cookies:

- **Session cookies:** Deleted when you log out
- **Preference cookies:** Retained 1 year
- **Analytics cookies:** Deleted per Google Analytics policy

## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### GDPR Rights (for EU users):

- **Right to access:** Request copy of your data
- **Right to correction:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to deletion:** Request your data deleted ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restrict:** Restrict how we use your data
- **Right to portability:** Get your data in machine-readable format
- **Right to object:** Object to data processing
- **Right to appeal:** Appeal our decisions

### California Privacy Rights (CCPA):

- **Right to know:** Request data we collect about you
- **Right to delete:** Request deletion of your data
- **Right to opt-out:** Opt-out of data sales (we don't sell)
- **Right to non-discrimination:** No penalties for exercising rights

### Other Rights:

- **Cookie control:** Enable/disable cookies in browser
- **Communication preferences:** Opt-out of emails
- **Data export:** Request your data in standard format
- **Account deletion:** Delete your account anytime

### How to Exercise Your Rights:

- Email: privacy@swapbuds.com
- Include: "Privacy request" in subject line
- Include: Your username and email address
- Include: Specific request (access, deletion, correction, etc.)
- Response time: Within 30 days (GDPR requirement)
- Verification: We may verify your identity

## 7. Data Security

### Protection Measures:

- **HTTPS encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted
- **TLS 1.2+:** Industry-standard encryption protocols
- **Database encryption:** Data at rest is encrypted
- **Password hashing:** Passwords stored with bcrypt hashing
- **API security:** Authentication tokens and rate limiting
- **Access controls:** Only authorized staff access data
- **Audit logging:** All access is logged
- **Firewalls:** Network protection from unauthorized access
- **Vulnerability testing:** Regular security audits
- **Incident response:** Plan for security breaches

### We are NOT responsible for:

- Losses due to password sharing or weak passwords
- Unauthorized access due to your negligence
- Loss of data from events beyond reasonable control
- Third-party security compromises

### Data Breaches:

- We will investigate any suspected breach
- Affected users will be notified without unreasonable delay
- Notification will include:
  - What data was compromised
  - Steps we took to secure data
  - Actions you should take
- We will comply with all legal notification requirements
